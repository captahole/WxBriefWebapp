Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WxBriefPro ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our website at https://www.wxbriefpro.com/.

What We Collect

WxBriefPro is a read-only weather briefing tool. We do not have user accounts, logins, or registration. The only data we collect is:

  • Usage Data — automatically collected by Google Analytics (GA4). This includes your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time on site, and device type. This data is used solely to understand how the site is used and to improve it.
  • Airport codes you enter — these are sent to our server to fetch weather data from third-party APIs (aviationweather.gov, datis.clowd.io, faa.gov). We do not store or log the airport codes you enter.
  • Recent airports (localStorage) — if you use the "Recent airports" feature, your last few airport combinations are saved locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Cookies

We use cookies only through Google Analytics to track aggregate site usage. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels beyond Google Analytics. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — for anonymous usage statistics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • aviationweather.gov — METAR and TAF data (FAA/NOAA public API).
  • datis.clowd.io — DATIS data.
  • faa.gov — airport status and delay information.

We have no control over the privacy practices of these third-party services.

Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Google Analytics retains usage data per its own retention settings (default 14 months). Local browser data (recent airports) is retained until you clear it or clear your browser storage.

Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to Other Websites

Our site may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
